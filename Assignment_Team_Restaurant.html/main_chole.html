Chole Bhature

529 calories – 12 grams of protein, 24 grams of fat, and 71 grams of carbohydrates
Description:

Chole puri features spicy, tangy chickpeas (chole) simmered with aromatic spices like cumin, coriander, and garam masala, served with fluffy, deep-fried puris.

Ingredients

Chickpeas (Kabuli Chana), Onions, Tomatoes, Ginger, Garlic, Green Chilies, Tea Bag, Bay Leaf, Cinnamon Stick, Cardamom Pods (Black and Green), Cloves, Cumin Seeds, Coriander Powder, Red Chilli Powder, Turmeric Powder

Serves: 1

Preparation Time: 20-25 min
