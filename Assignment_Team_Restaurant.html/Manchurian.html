Manchurian

294 calories – 12 grams of fat, 35 grams of carbs, and 5.9 grams of protein
Description:

Manchurian is a fusion dish, originating from the blend of Indian and Chinese culinary traditions. It features savory vegetable or meat balls, often including ingredients like cabbage, carrots, and spices, coated in a crispy batter and then deep-fried

Ingredients

Cabbage, Carrots, Capsicum (Bell Peppers), Spring Onions, Ginger, Garlic, Green Chilies, Cornflour, All-purpose flour (Maida), Soy Sauce, Vinegar, Sugar

Serves: 4 people

Preparation Time: 15-20 minutes
