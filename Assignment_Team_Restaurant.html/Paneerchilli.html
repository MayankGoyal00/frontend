Paneer Chilli

366 calories – 27g fat, 17g carbohydrates, 15g protein
Description:

Paneer chilli is a popular Indo-Chinese dish featuring crispy, batter-fried paneer cubes tossed in a flavorful, spicy, and slightly sweet and tangy sauce, often with bell peppers and onions.

Ingredients

Paneer (Indian Cottage Cheese), Capsicum (Bell Peppers), Onions, Green Chilies, Ginger, Garlic, Soy Sauce, Chilli Sauce, Tomato Ketchup, Vinegar, Cornflour, All-purpose flour (Maida), Spring Onions (for garnish), Vegetable Oil, Salt, Black Pepper

Serves: 4 people

Preparation Time: 20 minutes
