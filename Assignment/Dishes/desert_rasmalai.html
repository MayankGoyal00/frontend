Rasmalai

440 calories – 7.7g of fat, 33g of carbohydrates, 6g of protein, and 32g of sugar
Description:

Rasmalai, a popular Bengali sweet, features soft, spongy cottage cheese dumplings (chenna) soaked in a rich, creamy, and aromatic milk syrup flavored with cardamom and saffron, often garnished with nuts.

Ingredients:

Full-fat Milk, Sugar, Cardamom Powder, Saffron Strands, Chopped Nuts (Almonds, Pistachios)

Serves: 2

Preparation Time: 20-45 minutes
