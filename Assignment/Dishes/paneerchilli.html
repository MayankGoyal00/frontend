Paneer Chilli

366 calories – 27g fat, 17g carbohydrates, 15g protein
Description:

Paneer Lababdar, a rich and creamy North Indian dish, is made with paneer (Indian cottage cheese) simmered in a creamy tomato and cashew-based gravy, along with onions, spices, and herbs.

Ingredients

Paneer (Indian Cottage Cheese) Onions Tomatoes Cashews Poppy Seeds Ginger Garlic Green Chilies Cream Butter Red Chilli Powder Turmeric Powder Coriander Powder Garam Masala Kasuri Methi (Dried Fenugreek Leaves) Fresh Coriander Leaves (for garnish) Salt Vegetable Oil

Serves: 4 people

Preparation Time: 20 minutes
